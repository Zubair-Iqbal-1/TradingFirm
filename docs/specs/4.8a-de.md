## Decisions

### 1. Zones on the full stored history (data-engine)

`swing_snapshot` calls `support_resistance` on **all** daily bars; `pos_52w` keeps its 252-bar window (`WINDOW_52W` stays, now used by that field alone). `recent_bars` 20, `top_n` 3, `merge_pct` 0.5, `n_bins` 50, `top_nodes` 5 unchanged. **Known effect, not mitigated here:** two years of levels compete for three slots a side, ranked by score then distance, so a near, low-score zone can be displaced by a far, high-score one, and the 50 volume bins are wider over a 2-year range. The rerun's "v2 on fresh inputs" rows (decision 9) show the drift on the ten; if the nearest resistance vanishes on several, `top_n` is raised in the review, not silently here. Memory (G8): 501 bars, nothing to free beyond what the snapshot already does. `test_zones_use_full_history_not_52_weeks`, `test_pos_52w_still_uses_252_bars`.

### 2. `touches`, `held`, `broke`, `lastTouch` per zone (data-engine)

One pure function, `levels.zone_history(high, low, close, dates, zone_low, zone_high, hold_bars=3) → ZoneHistory(touches, held, broke, last_touch)`, run for every zone `support_resistance` returns, on the same full series. Definitions:

- **Band** = `[zone.low, zone.high]`. A bar **reaches** the band when its range overlaps it: `high ≥ zone.low and low ≤ zone.high`. A bar **jumps** the band when it does not reach it and its close is on the opposite side of the band from the previous valid close (a gap through the level with no bar inside it).
- **Episode** = a maximal run of consecutive reaching bars, or one jumping bar. One episode is one **touch**; a five-day consolidation at a level is one touch, not five.
- **Approach side** = the side (below / above the band) of the last valid close before the episode that lies outside the band. Approaching from below, the zone is being tested as resistance and the test is the bar's **high**; from above, as support, on its **low**. This is the user's definition applied per approach rather than per the zone's label today: over two years the same level is tested from both sides, and a level that rejected price both ways is the strongest kind. An episode with no such prior close (the series starts inside the band) is skipped.
- **Outcome** *(approval, change 1)*, judged on the closes from the episode's first bar e0 through the **first valid close after its last bar** (the window): **broke** if any close in the window lands on the far side (a far close beats an earlier approach-side close in the same episode: resistance 50.00–50.20 from below, day 1 high 50.10 close 49.80, day 2 low 49.90 high 50.60 close 50.50 is broke, not held); **held** only when no such close exists, the window is complete, and an approach-side close lands within e0 … e0+3 (`hold_bars`); else **undecided** (a slow rejection, or a window the series ends before completing: the current touch is never counted held until price has left the level). A jump bar closes on the far side, so it is broke by construction. `touches ≥ held + broke`. A jump needs the previous valid close outside the band; the bar directly after an episode is judged by the outcome rule, never as a new episode. *(approval, change 10)* Counts are side-agnostic, so a ceiling's `held` includes rejections from above (as support) and vice versa: accepted for v3 as a simplification and recorded in `decisions.md`; side-split counts are a later item if the journal shows the ceiling misfiring.
- **`lastTouch`** = the ISO date (`ts.date()`, no timezone conversion, CLAUDE.md bar rule) of the last bar of the newest episode; null when there is none.
- NaN: a bar with a NaN high, low or close never reaches or jumps, ends an episode, and its close is not a "valid close" for approach or outcome.
- A thin band (OUST's 26.29–26.30) means nearly every close is outside it, so the touch bar's own close decides: a wick into the level that closes below it is a hold; a close above is a break. That is the wanted semantics.

`ZoneOut` gains `touches: int = 0`, `held: int = 0`, `broke: int = 0`, `last_touch: Optional[str]` (alias `lastTouch`); `zone_to_dict` emits them; `Zone` gains the same four fields. Defaults let a cached pre-part body still validate (X11). Hand-computed on the 17-bar series of `test_levels.py`: `test_zone_history_counts_touch_held_broke`, `test_zone_history_touch_that_holds_on_its_own_close`, `test_zone_history_slow_rejection_is_undecided`, `test_zone_history_gap_through_band_is_a_break`, `test_zone_history_approach_side_flips`, `test_zone_history_skips_until_a_close_outside`, `test_zone_history_nan_bars_ignored`, `test_zone_history_empty_series`, `test_zone_history_far_close_in_episode_beats_earlier_hold`, `test_zone_history_open_episode_is_undecided`, `test_support_resistance_zones_carry_history` (the `Zone` equality in `test_support_resistance_splits_by_last_close_and_ranks_by_score` carries the history too); `test_indicators_endpoint.py` gains the four keys in its zone key-set assertion.

### 4. Plan math v3 — the stop: cent-floored components, most-held support, the swing low, and the extension flag

- **Cent-floored components (item 8):** `atr_c = to_cents(atr)`; every level input (zone low, EMA20, swing low) is floored to the cent before the subtraction: `stop = level_c − atr_c`, `disaster = stop − atr_c`, `entryForMaxRisk = stop + 2 × atr_c`. Every figure the basis prints is one of those components, so the printed subtraction always equals the level (`test_stop_built_on_cent_floored_components`, `test_basis_arithmetic_reconciles`: every `a - b` in a basis string evaluates to the level it names). Ratios (the 8-ATR cap, the 2-ATR far test, the extension test, `riskAtr` in the rerun) keep the **exact** ATR, as today: they are comparisons, not printed levels, and the cap test's OPCH numbers (7.00 ATR on 0.678571) stay. **OUST on this rule:** EMA20 36.60 − 2.02 = **34.58** (v2: 34.57), disaster 32.56, `entryForMaxRisk` 34.58 + 4.04 = **38.62**. The Next block's 38.61 was computed on v2's stop; 38.62 is what v3 prints.
- **Support zones within 2 ATR (item 7):** every support-side zone whose stop (`low_c − atr_c`) leaves `(entry − stop) ÷ ATR ≤ 2.00` (quantized half-up to 2 dp, boundary inclusive) is eligible; the one with the **highest `held`** wins, ties to the highest low (v2's rule; a zone without `held`, an older dossier, counts 0, so old inputs reproduce v2). The alternatives are never consulted when an eligible zone exists (v2's `test_near_support_never_uses_alternative_stop` stands). Worked: entry 50.00, ATR 1.20; P 49.00–49.10 held 1 → stop 47.80 (1.83 ATR); Q 48.80–48.90 held 4 → stop 47.60 (2.00 ATR, eligible) → **Q wins, stop 47.60**. With Q at 48.40–48.50 (stop 47.20, 2.33 ATR) Q is out and P wins. `test_stop_prefers_most_held_support_within_two_atr`, `test_stop_ignores_most_held_support_beyond_two_atr`, `test_stop_tie_on_held_takes_highest_low`, `test_stop_two_atr_boundary_inclusive`.
- **Far branch:** no eligible support zone → the stop is the highest of {the highest-low support zone's stop, `EMA20_c − atr_c`, `swing_c − atr_c`}, each valid only if its raw level ≤ entry and the result > 0 (v2's rule, the swing low now real: `test_swing_low_stop_wins_when_nearer`). None valid → `no_support`, unchanged.
- **Extension (item 6):** if the chosen stop still leaves `(entry − stop) ÷ ATR > 2.00`, the plan is built as usual and flagged `extended = True` with `entryForMaxRisk = to_cents(stop + FAR_SUPPORT_ATR × atr_c)`; otherwise `extended = False`, `entryForMaxRisk = None`. The flag never rejects: targets, R, size and `lossAtDisasterPct` are computed at the given entry as today (the size is small, that is the point). `test_extended_plan_carries_entry_for_max_risk` (OUST's numbers), `test_extended_never_when_risk_within_two_atr`, `test_extension_boundary_two_atr_inclusive`.

### 5. Plan math v3 — the ceiling (item 5)

Every resistance-side candidate that survives the 8-ATR cap is classed by its history:

- **Ceiling:** `held ≥ 3 and held ≥ 3 × broke` (so `broke = 0` always qualifies, `held 3 / broke 1` qualifies, `held 3 / broke 2` does not). The **nearest** ceiling is where the walk stops: candidates at or below it are eligible, the ceiling's own low is the **last eligible candidate** (a well-held level is exactly where profit is taken, so T1 may be the ceiling), nothing above it is a target. If no eligible candidate pays ≥ 1.5R → `PlanRejected("ceiling", "resistance 52.80-53.10, held 4, broke 0 caps the trade at 1.14R")`, a new `Reason`, checked after `no_target` and before `low_r`.
- **Looked through:** `broke ≥ held` (a level that gave way as often as it held): never a ceiling; listed as `overhead` when under 1.5R exactly as v2 does, the walk continuing.
- **Everything else** (no history, `held < 3`, or `held < 3 × broke` with `broke < held`): v2 behaviour, overhead under 1.5R, T1 at or above it.
- A zone dropped by the 8-ATR cap is neither target, overhead nor ceiling (it is gone before the walk). A zone straddling the entry can be a ceiling at its high (rare; `test_straddling_ceiling_rejects`).
- The basis text names it: `T1 53.50: resistance 53.50-53.80, touches 5, held 4, broke 0, last 2026-08-12, ceiling`. No new numeric field: the model reads `ceiling` in `basis` only. *(approval, change 7)* When a zone carries history the text prints `touches / held / broke / last` and drops the old `N tests` count, so a level reads one way; `tests` stays in the zone dict and in data-engine's scoring. `test_zone_history_replaces_tests_in_basis` (replacing 4.8a's `test_zone_history_fields_pass_through`, whose "decide nothing" no longer holds).

Worked *(approval, change 2: R recomputed)*: entry 50.00, stop 47.80 (risk 2.20). A 52.00 held 1 broke 2 (2.00 ÷ 2.20 = **0.91R**, looked through → overhead); B 53.50–53.80 held 4 broke 0 (3.50 ÷ 2.20 = **1.59R**, ceiling); C 56.00 held 2 broke 0 (6.00 ÷ 2.20 = **2.73R**). **v2:** overhead A, T1 B, T2 C. **v3:** overhead A, **T1 B, no T2** (C is beyond the ceiling). Variant: B at 52.80–53.10 (2.80 ÷ 2.20 = **1.27R**) → `PlanRejected("ceiling", "resistance 52.80-53.10, touches 5, held 4, broke 0 caps the trade at 1.27R")`. `test_ceiling_caps_t1`, `test_ceiling_under_min_r_rejects_ceiling`, `test_ceiling_ratio_rule`, `test_broken_zone_is_looked_through`, `test_zone_without_history_is_never_a_ceiling`, `test_capped_zone_is_never_a_ceiling`, `test_rejection_order` (updated).
