## Spec

Part 3.4b adds night checks. The scheduler gains a `night` slot every 30 minutes, at :15 and :45 ET. A slot exists whenever CME Globex equity futures trade and no XNYS session is running: weeknights 16:45 and 18:15 → 09:15 ET, Sundays from 18:15. A night check downloads `ES=F NQ=F`, then caps the latest settle's score by the futures' move since that settle, and never raises it:

| move | cap |
|---|---|
| −1.5 % | at most CAUTIOUS |
| −3 % | at most DANGER |
| −5 % | CRITICAL |

Market checks get the same cap, from the futures their 17-ticker download already carries (decision 5). Night rows go to `risk.health_checks` as kind `night` and publish through 3.4's throttle unchanged, so they reach 3.6b's regime trigger and the 07:30 brief's inputs.

It must **not**:
- build anything past step 0 before decision 3 is filled and v2 is approved
- run in the twin, add a flag, or change any compose service
- re-run the six monitors at night, change a 3.3 scoring rule, or change 3.4's throttle
- raise a score above the one it caps
- download when the check can't be stored or has nothing to compare against (no pool, no scored settle, a settle without futures), during the 17:00–18:00 ET halt, or while CME is closed
- keep a last-known futures body, or catch up a missed night slot
- change the news poller or data-engine (D12's night news is 3.5's around-the-clock poll)
- add a migration, write outside `risk`, or change 3.6a's inputs document beyond `health.overlay`
- touch prod except the G15 list

### 2. When a night check runs

- **Rule:** `night` slots are at ET minutes :15 and :45 when all three hold:
  1. `CMES` `is_open_on_minute`
  2. not `17:00 ≤ t < 18:00` ET: the daily halt, which `CMES` doesn't model (decision 10)
  3. not inside `[XNYS open, 16:20 settle]` of that ET date
  4. not within 45 min before an XNYS open, so **09:15 is never a night slot** and 08:45 is the last morning one (decision 7's cooldown rule). A date with no XNYS session has no open to stand clear of, so it keeps its 09:15.
- **Counts:**
  - Sunday: 12 (18:15–23:45)
  - Mon–Thu: 31 each (00:15–08:45 = 18, 16:45, 18:15–23:45 = 12)
  - Friday: 19 (the 18 morning slots, plus 16:45)
  - Saturday: 0
  - **155 a week**, one download each. The plan's §2 budget is "~30 a night".
- **Holidays**, as `CMES` models them:
  - Labor Day and Thanksgiving (no XNYS session, so no pre-open cut): 00:15–12:45 (26) and 18:15–23:45 (12) = 38
  - 11-27 and 12-24: the 18 morning slots only (CME closes 13:00)
  - 12-25: none
- **Why :15 / :45:** no night slot shares a minute with a brief slot (07:30 / 12:30 / 16:30), so the 07:30 brief reads a 15-minute-old 07:15 row. 08:45 is the last slot before the open (decision 7), and 16:45 the first after the settle.
- **DST:** both US switches fall at 02:00 on a Sunday morning, when CME is closed, so no night slot is ever ambiguous or skipped.
- **Merged, not parallel:** `slots_for_day(day)` returns market, settle and night slots in order. `slot_for`, `next_slot_after`, `last_slot_before`, the missed-slot count and 3.6a's expected-slot rule all see night slots with no other change. The grace stays 60 s, and a missed slot is never caught up.
- **`slots_for_day` is cached per ET date** (added during commit 2): night slots ask the futures calendar 48 times a day and `_missed_between` can call `next_slot_after` thousands of times. Both calendars are validated *before* the cache is read, so a rebuilt or out-of-range calendar is never served from it, and the cache is bounded at 60 dates (G8).
- **No new flag:** night slots run where `SCHEDULER_ENABLED` is true (prod only). The twin stays off. Rollback is the image tag.

### 4. The cap (provisional numbers, accepted at gate 1)

**Reference:** from 3.4b on, every check stores the futures price it saw, in its indicators JSONB:

`futures: {"ES=F": {price, date, asOf, stale}, "NQ=F": {…}}`

- It comes from the same download, with no extra request. A missing or stale ticker is `null`.
- The reference is the `futures` block of the check's settle base: the latest scored settle before the cutoff (decision 6).

**Move:** `movePct = (price − ref) × 100 / ref` per ticker, multiplied before dividing so the band edges are exact, as in `regime._pct`.
- The check's move is the lower (worse) of the two.
- Only one available → that one.
- A reference that is 0, negative, null or not finite → that ticker is unavailable, never an inf or a raise.

**Cap, first match:**

| move | cap | at most |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ −5.0 % | 19 | CRITICAL |
| ≤ −3.0 % | 39 | DANGER |
| ≤ −1.5 % | 69 | CAUTIOUS |
| otherwise | none | — |

- **`score = min(base, cap)`**, and the regime is `classify(score)`. `base` is the monitors' score (market) or the settle's score (night).
- **Why a cap, not a penalty:** each band maps to one regime, a move never compounds with an already-low base, and `min` can't raise a score by construction.
- **Not chosen:** additive penalties (−10 / −20 / −35), which can push a weak base into CRITICAL on a −3 % move.

**The overlay record** on the row, the payload and the endpoints:

`overlay: {status, movePct, esPct, nqPct, base, cap, capped}`

- `status: applied` when capped, `within` when the move is above −1.5 %
- `status: no_reference` when the settle base has no usable `futures`
- `status: unavailable` when there is no fresh current price
- the settle check stores `overlay: null` (decision 5)

**Provisional**, like 3.3's numbers: the three edges, the three caps and worse-of-two. Phase 6's journal retunes them without a spec correction.

### 5. Market checks get the same cap (chosen at gate 1; open question 1)

- **In the view:** a stale (last-known) or missing futures entry → `unavailable`, and the monitors' score stands. Futures refresh every 10 min, like VIX (open question 3, unchanged).

### 6. One night check, in order

`scheduler.run_check(state, "night")` hands off to `run_night_check(state, *, clock)`, so the loop and every loop-test patch keep one entry point.

1. **No pool** → skipped, WARNING, **no download** (never spend a request that can't be stored).
2. **Settle base:** `db.settle_reference(pool, now)` returns the latest scored settle before `now`: `checked_at, score, regime, indicators`.
   - `settle_cutoff(now, kind)` returns `now` for `night`, and the session open otherwise, as in 3.4.
   - At 20:15 Monday that is Monday's settle; at 07:15 Tuesday, still Monday's.
   - A read failure (`DB_FAILURES`, `TimeoutError`) → skipped, WARNING, no download.
   - None (a fresh database) → skipped, WARNING, no row.
3. **The settle has no usable `futures`** (every settle before 3.4b): no download. The row gets the settle's score, `overlay.status: no_reference`, `stale: true`.
4. **Quotes lock held** → skipped (3.4's carry-forward). Otherwise `quotes.get_night_quotes` (decision 7). A cooldown, refusal, error or empty answer → the row gets the settle's score, `unavailable`, `stale: true`.
5. **Health**, assembled from the settle row:
   - `monitors`, `coverage` and `staleMonitors` are copied unchanged, so `/market/indicators` and the inputs' monitor summary work as they do
   - `score` / `regime` come from the cap
   - `inputs` is the night download's `{asOf, source, reason, staleTickers}`
   - `futures` and `overlay` are set
   - `stale` is the settle's stale, or no usable price, or a settle base older than the latest session's settle (a missed settle)
6. **Trend** against the same settle → **publish** (`publish_health`, unchanged) → **hook** (3.6b's `on_check_published`) → **insert** (`insert_health_check`, kind `night`).
   - Each step is isolated exactly as in `run_check`. A Postgres failure never delays a publish.

### 7. The night download (`monitors/quotes.py`)

- **`get_night_quotes(r, memory, *, now)`:**
  - `NIGHT_TICKERS = ("ES=F", "NQ=F")`, with the interval and period from decision 3
  - key `risk_key("night_quotes")`, `TTL_NIGHT_QUOTES = 600` s, 120 s degraded
  - the same version guard, cooldown check, single-flight lock, log capture and `threads=False`, and no `session=`
- **Why 600 s:** under the 1,800 s slot, so every night slot downloads fresh. Open question 3's "cached every second slot" can't happen at night. A restart within 10 min reuses it.
- **Shared source cooldown:** a night refusal starts the `yfinance` cooldown, which also covers the next market check. One IP, one budget, by design.
- **Its length is `TTL_COOLDOWN_YFINANCE` = 900 s**, and it starts when the refusal is *detected*, not when the slot began. A 09:15 refusal is therefore still active at 09:30:00: `get_core_quotes` would raise `QuotesCoolingDown` and the session's first check would score from the 24 h last-known body instead of downloading. **That is why decision 2 drops 09:15.** The last morning slot, 08:45, clears by 09:00:30 at the worst — half an hour before the open.
- **No last-known key:** an hours-old futures price would be a wrong cap, not a stale-but-useful one.

### 10. The CMES calendar (G4, probed 2026-09-11)

`exchange_calendars` 4.13.2 is already pinned, so there's no dependency change. What the offline probe showed:
- **Timezone and session times:** `tz` is `America/Chicago`, with no breaks. Sessions open 18:00 ET the previous day and close 18:00 ET.
- **The halt isn't modelled, and neither is the Friday close:**
  - Mon 17:30 and **Fri 17:30 ET read as open**, although CME is halted from 17:00 and closed from Fri 17:00.
  - Decision 2's halt rule removes 17:15 / 17:45 every day.
- **Weekend:**
  - Sat 12:00 closed
  - Sun 17:45 closed, Sun 18:15 open
  - Sun 2026-11-01 18:15 (the DST end) open
- **Holidays:**
  - 09-07 and 11-26 close 13:00 ET and reopen 18:00
  - 11-27 and 12-24 close 13:00
  - 12-25 and 2027-01-01 closed
- **The calendar's holiday hours are taken as given.**
  - Wrongly "open": a night download returns the last trade, and the move is still real.
  - Wrongly "closed": a missing slot, logged as missed.
- **Code:** `futures_calendar(day)` is built lazily and cached like `market_calendar`, with the same rebuild-once-then-fail-closed rule. Out of bounds → no night slot plus ERROR; market slots are unaffected.
