## Spec

Part 4.3 adds two pure modules to `services/ai-agent`: `models/verdict.py`, the Pydantic shape of an analyst verdict and its trade plan (camelCase JSON, extra fields forbidden, ordering of levels enforced), and `grading/plan_math.py`, one function `compute_plan` that turns an entry, an ATR, the dossier's zones, an account size and a risk percent into either a plan (stop = nearest support zone low − 1×ATR, disaster line = stop − 1×ATR, targets = the next resistance zones above entry with their R, size = floor(account × risk% ÷ (entry − stop))) or a named rejection — best R under 1.5 among them. It must **not** call an LLM, read config, touch Redis, Postgres or the network, add a route, invent a price level that did not come from a zone or the ATR, raise on data that can legitimately be missing (no ATR, no zones), apply a migration, change compose, or rebuild/deploy anything. **Acceptance:** `tests/test_plan_math.py` and `tests/test_verdict_model.py` green in `tf-ai-agent-dev` with every hand-checked number below asserted exactly and every failure-branch test passing by name; data-engine 490, risk-shield 857 and ai-agent's existing 236 unchanged, plus the new tests, ai-agent at ≥ 99 % coverage with 100 % on both new modules; a one-shot run in the twin printing the worked example's plan as actual output (G2); both test files in `.github/workflows/tests.yml` (its own `chore:` commit, pushed by the user) and then in CLAUDE.md.

**Normalization:** no keys exist. The one shared normalization is for numbers: `to_decimal(x)` = `Decimal(str(x))`, applied to entry, ATR, every zone low/high, account and risk_pct **before any arithmetic**. Every subtraction, division and floor runs in Decimal; only the final numbers go back to float. `to_cents(d)` floors a Decimal to 0.01; every stop, disaster line and target goes through it. R goes through `r_multiple(target, entry, stop)`, rounded half-up to 2 dp.

**Check order** (the first failing check wins, `test_rejection_order`): arguments (entry, account, risk_pct, every zone) → ATR → support → stop > 0 → disaster > 0 → targets → best R → size.

## Decisions

1. **Settled before this spec (2026-09-21):** `account` and `risk_pct` are **function parameters** of `compute_plan`, not a database read. 4.4 wires them to `users.settings` (seeded by migration `005_ai.sql`).
2. **`risk_pct` is a percent**, matching `users.settings.risk_per_trade_pct`: `1.0` means 1 %. Valid range `(0, 10]`; outside it is a `ValueError`.
3. **Long plans only.** Stop below entry, targets above. Shorts are not modelled (D6/D11 describe long swing entries).
4. **Zones are pooled and re-split around the entry** (V2). The caller passes `support + resistance` as one list; plan math ignores data-engine's split, because 4.4's `entry=` can differ from the last close. Support candidates are zones with `low ≤ entry`; target candidates are zones with `low > entry`. A zone straddling the entry is a support candidate. Only the zones the dossier carries are seen (top 3 each side by score) — a nearer, weaker zone can be missing; that is data-engine's cut, not re-derived here.
5. **"Nearest support" = the candidate with the highest `low`.** Stop = `to_cents(low − 1×ATR)`.
6. **Disaster line is computed from the rounded stop**: `to_cents(stop − 1×ATR)`, so the printed numbers subtract exactly.
7. **A target's price is the zone's `low`, floored to the cent** — the first price at which resistance starts, the conservative choice. (Alternative: the zone's `price`, its member mean; it raises every R slightly.) Targets are the up-to-3 nearest candidates, ascending.
8. **R is rounded to 2 dp and the 1.5 test uses the rounded value**, so a plan printed with `R 1.50` is never rejected. Best R = the largest R among the targets.
9. **No resistance above entry is a rejection (`no_target`)**, not an invented target. A stock at an all-time high therefore gets no plan. This is recorded in `docs/decisions.md` as an **open gap**, not a design decision: a measured-move or ATR-multiple target is unscoped.
10. **Size has three bounds (D11):** `size = min(risk sizing, cash cap, max position)` with risk sizing `floor(account × risk_pct/100 ÷ (entry − stop))`, cash cap `floor(account ÷ entry)`, max position `floor(account × MAX_POSITION_PCT/100 ÷ entry)`, `MAX_POSITION_PCT = 25`. Without them a tight stop sizes a position larger than the account (10,000 at 2 % risk, stop 0.15 away, entry 50 → 1,333 shares = 66,650). `size_bound` (`"risk"`, `"max position"`, `"cash cap"`, ties in that order) and `size_basis` name which bound applied. The plan row does not have the two caps; they go to `docs/decisions.md` as a plan correction.
11. **`entry` is added to the plan.** The row's plan has no entry, but R cannot be checked without it. Also a plan correction in `docs/decisions.md`.
12. **Results are plain frozen dataclasses**, not the Pydantic model: `PlanMath(entry, stop, stop_basis, disaster_line, targets: tuple[Target(price, r)], best_r, risk_per_share, risk_budget, size_shares, size_bound, size_basis)` or `PlanRejected(reason, detail)`. `reason` is one of the seven codes in the table. 4.4 merges `PlanMath` with the model's invalidation / earnings / horizon into `models.verdict.Plan`.
13. **Constants, not env knobs:** `STOP_ATR_MULT = 1.0`, `DISASTER_ATR_MULT = 1.0`, `MIN_BEST_R = 1.5`, `MAX_TARGETS = 3`, `MAX_POSITION_PCT = 25`, module-level in `plan_math.py`. Plan math reads no config.
14. **Verdict schema** (`models/verdict.py`, snake_case fields with camelCase aliases, `populate_by_name`, `extra="forbid"`):
    - `Verdict`: `verdict: "go" | "wait" | "avoid"`, `confidence: int 0–100`, `reasoning: str 1–2000`, `thesis: list[str]` **exactly 3**, each 1–300, `thesis_breakers: list[str]` 1–6, each 1–300, `plan: Plan | None`, `risk_flags: list[str]` 0–8, each 1–100. **`go` requires a plan**; `wait` / `avoid` may carry one or `None`.
    - `Plan`: `entry`, `stop`, `stop_basis: str`, `disaster_line`, `invalidation: str 1–300`, `targets: list[Target]` 1–3, `size_shares: int ≥ 1`, `size_basis: str`, `earnings_in_days: int ≥ 0 | None` (None = no known date), `hold_through_earnings: bool = False`, `horizon_days: int 1–60`. Validator: `0 < disaster_line < stop < entry < targets[0] < … ` strictly ascending.
    - `Target`: `price > 0`, `r > 0`.
    - The length caps are provisional; 4.4 tunes them against real answers. The LLM-facing structured-output schema (strict mode needs every field required) is 4.4's, derived from this model; 4.3 does not build it.
15. **Commits:** `feat: verdict schema` (this spec, `models/`, its test) and `feat: plan math` (`grading/plan_math.py`, its test) instead of the row's one; then `chore:` for `.github/workflows/tests.yml` alone — shared prod code, with per-block SHA-256 proof for the untouched blocks, pushed by the user, not by me; then `docs:` (CLAUDE.md test list, progress row, overview, the decisions entries for 7/10/11 and the open gap in 9).

## Worked examples (asserted exactly in `test_plan_math.py`)

**A — accepted.** entry 50.00, ATR 1.20, account 25,000, risk 1 %. Zones: 45.00–45.40, 47.80–48.10, 53.90–54.30, 57.50–58.00.
Nearest support low 47.80 → stop **46.60**; disaster **45.40**; risk/share 3.40. Targets **53.90 (R 1.15)**, **57.50 (R 2.21)**; best 2.21 ≥ 1.5. Size floor(250 ÷ 3.40 = 73.53) = **73**, basis risk; max position 125 and cash cap 500, not binding. (In float, 47.80 − 1.20 = 46.599999999999994 would floor to 46.59; the Decimal-first rule is what makes this 46.60.)

**B — rejected `low_r`.** A without the 57.50 zone → best R 1.15.

**C — max position.** entry 50.00, ATR 0.05, support low 49.90, target 58.00, account 10,000, risk 2 % → stop **49.85**, risk sizing 1,333, cash cap 200, max position **50** → size 50, basis "max position".

**D — rounding.** support low 47.803, ATR 1.2047 → stop **46.59**, disaster **45.38**.
