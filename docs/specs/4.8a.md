## Spec

Part 4.8a-ai rewrites `grading/plan_math.py` as **plan math v2**: T1 is the **first resistance zone low above entry paying ≥ 1.5R** and nearer zones are listed as `overhead` (price + R) instead of targets; any target more than `8×ATR14` above the entry (distance quantized to 2 dp) is dropped; when the support-zone stop leaves a risk over `2×ATR14` (or there is no support zone at all) the stop moves to **EMA20 − 1×ATR** when that is nearer (the swing-low kwargs exist for 4.8a-de, None until then); a zone straddling the entry is overhead (resistance side) or the stop zone (support side); every level carries a plain-language `basis` naming its zone, all numbers in cents; the plan carries `lossAtDisasterPct` and size never exceeds a 2.5 % loss at the disaster line; and every verdict is stamped with `plan_math_version` (migration `009_plan_math_version.sql`, in the fingerprint, and `GET /journal/stats` splits every group by model × version, with a stop-hit rate by risk-in-ATR bucket). It also delivers the **free diagnostic**: a no-LLM rerun of v1 and v2 over the ten stored verdicts' dossiers, reported as a table. It must **not**: make any LLM call, change `prompts/verdict.md` beyond the one legend line for `overhead` / `basis`, change the classifier, events or the prompt's rules (4.8b); let the model supply any number (the LLM schema is untouched); change `journal/scoring.py`'s rule (`target_hit` on `targets[0]`, which by X1 now becomes T1 by construction); edit `007_ai.sql` or `008_journal.sql`; touch data-engine; write outside the `ai` schema and `tf:ai:`; apply a prod migration or rebuild any prod service; or print an account size, a share count, a risk budget or the loss percent in the rerun. **Acceptance:** the ai-agent twin green, 0 skipped, every failure-branch test below passing by name; the rerun table over the ten (v1 vs v2: stop rule, stop, risk in ATR, overhead count, T1 price + R, valid / rejection reason) shown as actual output and reviewed before any deploy step; `PROJECTION_VERSION` 3 and a `test_projection_version_is_pinned` update; a twin round trip on `tradingfirm_dev` (009 applied by `scripts/dev-db.sh`) storing a v2 plan and reading back `plan_math_version = 2`, `prompt_inputs.planMathVersion`, `overhead` and the basis strings; and the prod migration + rebuild listed as waiting for a go with their timing.

## Decisions

### 1. T1 = the first zone paying ≥ 1.5R; nearer zones are `overhead`

- Candidates: every resistance-side zone low > entry (decision 7 adds a straddling zone's high), floored to the cent, ascending, de-duplicated (as today).
- Walk ascending. A candidate with R < `MIN_BEST_R` (1.5) is **overhead**; the first with R ≥ 1.5 is **T1**, and it and the next two candidates are `targets` (max 3, as today). *(approval)* **After three overhead entries the walk continues** to find T1; only the list is truncated to the nearest three (`test_overhead_capped_at_three`). In practice data-engine sends at most 3 resistance zones (X5), so overhead + targets ≤ 3 and the cap never binds; recorded, no change.
- No candidate at all → `no_target` (unchanged). Candidates exist but none pays ≥ 1.5R within the cap → `low_r` (reason name kept: the model's `no plan: low_r` flag and every stored row keep their meaning; `detail` = `no resistance pays >= 1.5R: best R 1.15`).
- `overhead` is a new list on `PlanMath` (`tuple[Target, ...]`, may be empty), on `plan_view` (the model reads it), on `Plan` (`overhead: list[Target] = []`, max 3; *(approval)* the validator requires `entry < overhead[i] < T1`, ascending), and in `plan_proposed`. **Not** on the LLM schema (no model field). One legend sentence in `prompts/verdict.md` names `overhead` and `basis`; `prompt_sha` becomes `4728f564ef292bda`: re-check `cacheWrite` after deploy (CLAUDE.md rule).
- `bestR` stays (R of the best target).
- **Effect on the journal (decisions 2026-09-22, second bullet):** `target_hit` is judged on `targets[0]` unchanged in code, and `targets[0]` is now T1 ≥ 1.5R. Rows stamped version 1 keep their stored plans; stats split by version (decision 4) so the two eras are never one rate.

### 2. Target distance cap: `TARGET_MAX_ATR = 8` *(review 2026-09-23: 8, picked from the 6 / 7 / 8 tables; approval had 6)*

A candidate whose distance in ATRs, `atr_distance = (price − entry) ÷ ATR14` **quantized half-up to 2 dp like R**, exceeds 8 is dropped, before the T1 walk, so a capped zone is neither target nor overhead. Quantizing first is the point: OPCH's 28.70 sat 4.7500 above a 23.95 entry and a raw float compare against 7 × 0.678571 = 4.749998 dropped a level that prints as 7.00 ATR. Scale-free of the stop, so a tight stop cannot push a real zone over the cap the way an R cap would. VITL's 30.48 target on a 12.23 entry (18.25 above) is dropped for any ATR below 2.28 (18.25 ÷ 8). The boundary is inclusive: exactly 8.00 ATR is kept, 8.01 dropped (`test_target_cap_boundary_inclusive`, which also holds the quantized case 29.38 = 8.00 kept / 29.39 = 8.02 dropped on OPCH's ATR). All candidates dropped → `no_target` with detail `every resistance above entry 50.0 is beyond 8xATR 9.60: 60.00, 70.00` (no new reason enum). At 6 and 7 the ten gave v2 5 / 10 (OPCH `no_target`); at 8, 6 / 10 with OPCH on the EMA20 stop at 6.33R; no other ticker has a zone between 6 and 8 ATR. The number is provisional like 3.3's, retuned from journal stats. Rejected alternative: an R cap.

### 3. The far-support stop *(approval: source = EMA20 alone; trigger = risk > 2×ATR)*

- **Trigger:** the risk the support-zone stop would take, `entry − (zone low − 1×ATR)`, is more than `FAR_SUPPORT_ATR = 2` ATRs. It measures the **stop distance**, not the distance to the zone low (which is 1 ATR less). *(approval)* **No support zone below the entry counts as far support** (risk = ∞): the alternative is consulted; if it is invalid the answer is `no_support` as today (`test_rejects_no_support_below_entry` covers both branches; `test_rejection_order` has the case).
- **Candidates when triggered:** `EMA20 − 1×ATR`, and `swing_low − 1×ATR` once 4.8a-de sends `lastSwingLow` (until then the kwargs are None; `analyst.py` already passes `indicators.get("lastSwingLow")` through, so plan math needs no edit when the field arrives — `test_analyze_passes_swing_low_and_ema20_when_present`, `test_missing_swing_low_falls_back_to_ema20`). Each is valid only if its raw level ≤ entry (so risk ≥ 1 ATR) and the result > 0. The stop is the **highest** of the valid candidates **and the zone stop**, so the alternative can only tighten the risk, never widen it (`test_far_support_without_alternative_keeps_zone_stop`). Same 1×ATR buffer as the zone rule (`STOP_ATR_MULT`): one constant governs every stop.
- **Worked example** *(approval: reconciled)*: entry 50.00, ATR 1.20, support zone 48.20–48.20, resistance 56.00. Zone stop 47.00. Distances: entry − zone low = 1.80 (1.50 ATR); entry − zone stop = **3.00 = 2.50 ATR**, which is what the trigger measures: > 2.40 → far. EMA20 49.00 → 47.80 (risk 2.20 = 1.83 ATR). Swing low 48.50 (4.8a-de) → 47.30. The higher wins: **stop 47.80**, disaster 46.60, T1 56.00 at R = 6.00 ÷ 2.20 = **2.73**. Without the alternative the zone stop stays: 47.00, R = 6.00 ÷ 3.00 = 2.00. With EMA20 at 50.50 (above the entry) it is invalid and the swing low's 47.30 wins. `stop_basis`: `stop 47.80: EMA20 49.00 - 1xATR 1.20; support 48.20-48.20 gives risk 3.00 = 2.50 ATR (> 2 ATR)`.
- **Not far:** support zone 49.00 → stop 47.80, risk 2.20 = 1.83 ATR: the alternatives are never consulted, even when EMA20 would give a tighter 48.70 (`test_near_support_never_uses_alternative_stop`).
- Disaster line = stop − 1×ATR, unchanged.
- `compute_plan` gains `ema20`, `swing_low`, `swing_low_date` keyword args (None allowed; a non-number is a `ValueError` like every caller bug).

### 4. `plan_math` version stamped and split *(approval: settled)*

No backfill `UPDATE`; reads `COALESCE(plan_math_version, 1)`.
- `db.insert_verdict` writes it (24th column, pinned by `test_migrations_hold_every_verdict_column_db_py_writes`); `db.journal_rows` selects the coalesced version plus `entry`, `plan_proposed->>'stop'` and the ATR the row was built on (`atr14Usd`, or `atr14` on rows before the units fix) for decision 5's buckets.
- Also stored inside `prompt_inputs` as `planMathVersion`.
- **Fingerprint:** `"planMath": PLAN_MATH_VERSION` joins the basis (`test_plan_math_version_in_fingerprint`).
- **`GET /journal/stats`:** `models[]` is one entry per **model × planMathVersion**, `{model, planMathVersion, byVerdict, calibration, stopHitByRiskAtr}`; rates never cross a version, mirroring "never mix models" (4.5 decision 13).
- `PROJECTION_VERSION` → **3** (`planMathVersion`, `plan.overhead`, `plan.*.basis`: 56 → 58 key paths).

### 5. Stats by risk-in-ATR bucket *(approval, change 9)*

`stopHitByRiskAtr[horizon][bucket] = {n, stopHitRate}` per model × version entry, buckets `<1.5` / `1.5-2` / `>2` on `(entry − stop) ÷ ATR14` (1.5 and 2 fall in the middle bucket). A plan-less row, an unscored horizon, or a row with no positive ATR or risk counts in no bucket. `journal.stats.risk_atr` / `risk_bucket` are the one normalization pair. `test_stats_by_risk_bucket`.

### 6. `basis` on every level, in cents *(approval, change 6; verdict-units decision 6)*

*(review 2026-09-23)* A zone's `held`, `broke` and `lastTouch` — the history fields 4.8a-de will send, None today — pass through to the basis text untouched (`3 tests, held 3, broke 0, last 2026-08-12`) and decide nothing here; a missing or malformed field is not printed. The ceiling rule that reads them is plan math v3, in 4.8a-de. `test_zone_history_fields_pass_through`.

Every target and overhead entry carries `basis`: `T1 56.90: resistance 56.90-57.30, 3 tests, volume node, score 85`, `overhead 53.90: resistance 53.90-54.30, 2 tests, score 50`, `overhead 50.40: zone high, resistance 49.70-50.40, 2 tests, score 50 straddles entry 50.00`. The stop's is `stop 46.60: support 47.80-48.10, 4 tests, score 65, low 47.80 - 1xATR 1.20`. `tests`, `volumeNode` and `score` are named when the zone has them (a zone with `low` and `high` only still has a basis). Every number in the text is floored to the cent, the same rounding as the levels (`to_cents`; review 2026-09-23: the first build rounded the text half-up, so OUST's T1 49.38 named a zone "49.39-49.50"; `test_level_and_basis_share_rounding`). A printed subtraction of two floored figures can still sit one cent above the floored exact difference; the level printed first is the plan's, and computing the stop on cent-floored inputs is a plan math v3 item. Text only: no number the model can set; `Target.basis` is optional on read so rows stored before 4.8a still parse (`test_plan_before_4_8a_still_parses`). `test_level_basis_names_zone`, `test_basis_text_is_in_cents`.

### 7. Zones straddling the entry *(approval, change 7; review 2026-09-23: the label, not a proxy)*

Each zone's side is **data-engine's own label**: the analyst tags every zone with the list it came from (`analyze.tagged_zones` → `side: "support" | "resistance"`), and plan math classifies by `side` (`_Zone.is_support`). The midpoint against the entry is only the **fallback for a zone without a side** (older stored dossiers in the rerun); an unknown side is a `ValueError` like a broken zone. The first report described the midpoint as the rule; it was a proxy, and on the ten the proxy and the label agree (IAG 20.11–20.20 and MSFT 500.92–503.17 are the two straddling zones, both labelled resistance), so the rerun table did not move. `test_zone_side_from_label_beats_midpoint`. A **resistance** zone with `low ≤ entry ≤ high` is overhead, listed first, at price = zone high with its R, whatever that R is; everything up to that high is overhead too, and a plan with nothing above it is `low_r` ("no target outside the zone straddling entry"). One with midpoint < entry is a **support** zone: the stop zone (its low − 1×ATR), consulted before any zone below it. A zone whose low equals the entry is resistance (midpoint ≥ entry), as data-engine treats `price == close`. `test_resistance_zone_straddling_entry_is_overhead`, `test_support_zone_straddling_entry_is_stop_zone`, `test_zone_low_equal_to_entry_is_overhead`.

### 8. `lossAtDisasterPct` and the 2.5 % size cap *(approval, change 8)*

A fourth size bound, `disaster loss` = ⌊account × 2.5 % ÷ (entry − disaster)⌋, joins risk, max position and cash cap (ties in that order). `lossAtDisasterPct = size × (entry − disaster) ÷ account × 100`, 2 dp, on `PlanMath`, `Plan` (optional on read) and `plan_proposed`; `size_basis` names the bound and the percent. It is **not** in `plan_view`: a percent of the account is still about the account, which the model never sees (`riskBudget` precedent). The rerun script never prints it. Worked example: entry 50.00, ATR 2.00, stop 45.80, disaster 43.80, risk 3 % of 25,000: risk 750 ÷ 4.20 = 178, max position 125, cash 500, **disaster loss 625 ÷ 6.20 = 100** → 100 shares, loss 100 × 6.20 ÷ 25,000 = 2.48 %. `test_size_capped_by_disaster_loss`.
